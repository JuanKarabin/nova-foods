# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS (no frameworks), one folder per demo (`demo-1` … `demo-4`), images referenced from `../assets/`. Deploy target: Netlify (contact form uses Netlify Forms; must also be testable locally before deploy).

## Users

Purchasing managers and owners of food-service businesses: pizzerias, caterers, restaurants, gastronomic chains. They are evaluating a supplier for white-label (marca blanca) products they will sell under their own brand, and need to trust consistency, volume capacity and logistics before requesting a quote.

## Product Purpose

NOVA FOODS ARGENTINA SA is an industrial food plant that manufactures white-label products for other businesses: raw materials/premixes, prepizzas, bollos (dough balls), and empanadas/fillings. The institutional one-page exists to present the company and convert visitors into B2B quote requests.

## Positioning

Strictly B2B. The client's brand goes on the product; NOVA supplies the manufacturing, standardization and volume behind it. Plant located in Quilmes (Amoedo y Vicente López), south of the Buenos Aires metro area.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, search or sales outreach, compare suppliers, and act through a quote form (Nombre, Empresa, Rubro, Teléfono, Email, Volumen estimado). Logistics (van, truck, boxed shipments) is part of the offer as shown in the brand applications.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product lines: materia prima, prepizzas, bollos, empanadas y rellenos.
- Plant location: Amoedo y Vicente López, Quilmes, Buenos Aires (Google Maps embed provided by the user).
- No figures confirmed (m², capacity, years, certifications): the user chose to use qualitative copy only. Do not invent numbers or seals.
- Client logos not available yet: aesthetic placeholders, clearly generic.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NOVA FOODS · ARGENTINA SA.
- Brand manual (`assets/NOVA FOODS - Manual de Marca.pdf`): monochrome identity — black, white, grays #58595A and #2D2B2C. Logo in plain, filled-badge (wavy-edged plate) and outlined-badge variants, plus negative versions.
- Typography: Abolition Regular (display, "NOVA FOODS"), Lexend Deca (secondary, "ARGENTINA SA", body).
- Accent: workwear blue taken from the manual's applications (cap, jacket), confirmed by the user as the brand accent.
- Tone: professional, reliable, robust, corporate.

## Evidence on Hand

- `assets/logo.png` (filled badge, black), `assets/cajas.png`, `assets/camion.png`, `assets/camioneta.png`, `assets/camiseta.png` (brand mockups).
- No testimonials, customers, figures or certifications. Must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. The client's brand is the hero; NOVA is the reliable plant behind it.
2. Consistency over novelty: every batch identical, said plainly.
3. Make the quote request the obvious next step from any section.
4. Never claim what isn't confirmed.
